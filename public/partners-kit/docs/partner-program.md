# Seraphim Scan Partner Program

## Overview
Seraphim Scan runs Scoped Assessments for teams building and shipping AI agents. Partners introduce us to teams that would benefit, and we handle the engagement end to end: passive recon of public information, a Scoped Assessment within an agreed scope and written client authorization, then remediation support and a retest.

## Who it is for
- Agencies and consultancies that build AI products for clients
- Fractional CTOs, advisors and investors with portfolio companies shipping agents
- Developer tool and platform companies whose customers deploy agents

## How it works
1. Apply and get your partner link and materials.
2. Introduce a team using the templates below, or share your link.
3. We run a short intro call. Nothing beyond passive recon happens without written client authorization and an agreed scope.
4. When the engagement is signed, your referral is credited.
