# Pitch blurb (about 50 words)
Seraphim Scan runs Scoped Assessments for teams shipping AI agents. We start with passive recon of public information, assess only what you authorize in writing, and finish with remediation support and a retest. You get a clear view of what your agents can reach and proof that fixes hold.
