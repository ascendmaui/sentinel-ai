# Brand usage for partners
- Name: "Seraphim Scan", two words, both capitalized.
- Use the supplied logo files only. Do not redraw, recolor, stretch or add effects.
- Gold mark on dark backgrounds; the light and mono versions are for light or single-color contexts.
- Keep clear space equal to the aperture ring diameter around the logo.
- Minimum: horizontal lockup 120 px wide; below that use the symbol.
- Say "Scoped Assessment". Recon is passive only. No em dashes. No invented statistics or client logos.
- Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque (display) and Inter (body), both free on Google Fonts.
- Colors: Obsidian #07070C, Ivory #F3EEE3, Sovereign Gold #F2B544.
