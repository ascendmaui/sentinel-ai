# FAQ
**What is a Scoped Assessment?** A security review limited to systems and methods the client agrees to in writing. It starts with passive recon of public information.

**Is this a pentest?** We describe the service as a Scoped Assessment. Any active testing happens only inside the agreed scope with written authorization.

**What does passive recon cover?** Publicly available information: DNS records, certificates, published docs, public repositories and what the product shows any visitor.

**How long does it take?** It depends on scope. We confirm timing on the intro call.

**What does it cost?** Pricing tiers are on the site. Custom scopes are quoted after the intro call.
