## Short

Seraphim Scan, an Ascend Maui initiative, runs scoped security assessments for teams that ship AI agents. The work has three phases: passive recon of public information, a Scoped Assessment inside a written authorization, and remediation with a retest. Halo Scan, the priced starting point, is a $99 one-time passive sweep of one authorized domain, with each finding checked twice and delivered as a PDF.

## Standard

Seraphim Scan helps teams that ship AI agents see the exposure on their public surface and, under a written scope, assess the systems they name. Engagements follow three phases. Recon uses public information only. A Scoped Assessment, including scenario design on private local models, stays inside the agreed boundaries. Remediation and Retest ranks fixes and checks that they landed.

Halo Scan is $99, flat. It covers DNS and mail authentication, TLS, security headers, homepage cookies, and public certificate-transparency names for the single domain the buyer authorizes. Findings that do not reproduce on a second pass are dropped. The branded PDF is usually emailed within minutes of payment and always within 3 business days. It is not an active test, not an AI-agent review, and not a certification.

Higher tiers, named Seraphim, Cherubim, Thrones, and Angels, are custom quotes sent after the scan. Buying the fixes is optional. The company does not sell a monitoring platform, sensors, or a compliance certificate.
